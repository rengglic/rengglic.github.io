I am a Senior Researcher and Lecturer at [ETH](https://inf.ethz.ch)'s [Systems Group](https://systems.ethz.ch), working with [Ana Klimovic](https://anakli.inf.ethz.ch).

My research lies at the intersection of data management and AI/ML systems. I mainly study the data that AI systems consume and produce, and how to manage it so that these systems require less compute and data while the quality of their results stays measurable, ideally with statistical guarantees. During my PhD at ETH Zurich, I focused on the quality of static datasets, through [feasibility studies based on Bayes error estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.08410) and [continuous integration of ML models with statistical guarantees](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.00278) at minimal labelling cost. At Apple, I turned to operational data, leading the design of on-device vector search and studying [LLM-based access to relational data (Text2SQL)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18197). Today, I focus on the interaction traces that AI agents generate at runtime: managing them as a new class of data, and using them to build workload-aware serving systems, estimate usage with statistical guarantees, and control cost and quality in unreliable execution environments.

Previously, I was a Senior Researcher at [Apple](https://www.apple.com) and a PostDoc at UZH ([DaST](https://www.ifi.uzh.ch/en/dast.html), with [Dan Olteanu](https://www.ifi.uzh.ch/en/dast/people/Olteanu.html)). I received my PhD from [ETH](https://inf.ethz.ch)'s [Systems Group](https://systems.ethz.ch), advised by [Ce Zhang](https://zhangce.github.io).
