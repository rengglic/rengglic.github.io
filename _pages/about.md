I am a Senior Researcher and Lecturer at [ETH](https://inf.ethz.ch)'s [Systems Group](https://systems.ethz.ch), working with [Ana Klimovic](https://anakli.inf.ethz.ch).

My research lies at the intersection of data management and ML/AI systems. A recurring theme in my work is the cost vs. quality trade-off in AI systems: reducing the data and compute they require, while keeping the quality of their results measurable, ideally with statistical guarantees. During my PhD at ETH Zurich, I developed data-centric methods for this trade-off across the ML lifecycle, from [feasibility studies based on Bayes error estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.08410) before development begins, to [continuous integration of ML models with statistical guarantees](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.00278) at minimal labelling cost. At Apple, I led the design of on-device vector search and studied [LLM-based access to relational data (Text2SQL)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18197). Today, I work on workload-aware serving and post-training for AI agents, and treat their interaction traces as a new class of data management artefacts.

Previously, I was a Senior Researcher at [Apple](https://www.apple.com) and a PostDoc at UZH ([DaST](https://www.ifi.uzh.ch/en/dast.html), with [Dan Olteanu](https://www.ifi.uzh.ch/en/dast/people/Olteanu.html)). I received my PhD from [ETH](https://inf.ethz.ch)'s [Systems Group](https://systems.ethz.ch), advised by [Ce Zhang](https://zhangce.github.io).
